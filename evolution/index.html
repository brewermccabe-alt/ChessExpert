    Rules
    • Goal: capture the enemy King. There is no check.
    • Pockets: each army starts in its own 2×8 pocket, an extension attached to the edge of the big board. Step out through the front, and defend your pocket: it is where your King lives.
    • Home base: when one of your pieces is captured, it waits to return. On your turn, pick it in the side panel and click any free square in your pocket to place it there for free. It comes back as the same kind of piece but with no upgrades, and it can capture right away. If your pocket is full, it waits for room. Captured Kings do not return: the game ends. The Knight's Extra life is the one exception (see below).
    • Turns: you get several actions per turn (board width ÷ 8, at least 3). Each piece can act once per turn. Buying upgrades is free.
    • Money: every move earns $50 and every capture earns $150, into your bank. Select a piece and buy its next upgrade. Each piece has one fixed upgrade path, bought in order; upgrades are kept. A gold glow means a piece can afford its next upgrade.
    • Firing: some upgrades capture without moving (a 🎯 button appears on the piece). Firing uses one action, ignores pieces in the way, and earns $150.
    • Freezing: a frozen piece cannot move or fire. It lasts for the frozen side's next turn only.
    • Sliding pieces (Bishop, Rook, Queen) reach up to 8 squares in one move on the huge board.
    • Pawns move 1 (2 from their starting row) and capture diagonally. No en passant.
