    Rules
    • Goal: capture the enemy King. There is no check.
    • Turns: you get several actions per turn (board width ÷ 8, at least 3). Each piece can act once per turn. Evolving is free.
    • XP: capturing earns XP equal to the victim's value (Pawn 1, Knight/Bishop 2, Rook/Nightrider 3, Queen 5, Amazon 7). Pawns also earn 1 XP for every 6 squares they march.
    • Evolution: when a piece has enough XP (gold glow), select it and pick what it becomes.
      • Pawn (2 XP) → Knight or Bishop
      • Knight / Bishop (3 XP) → Rook or Nightrider
      • Rook / Nightrider (5 XP) → Queen
      • Queen (8 XP) → Amazon (queen + knight)
    • Range: sliding pieces have a limited reach that grows as they evolve: Bishop 6, Rook 8, Queen 10, Amazon 12. A Nightrider repeats its knight jump up to 4 times.
    • Pawns move 1 (2 from the start), capture diagonally. No en passant.
    • View: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click the minimap to jump.
